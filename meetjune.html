meet june

woof! you found me! 🐾 i'm june, the real star of this website. my hobbies include chasing tennis balls, demanding attention, and occasionally listening to my human. anyway, thanks for stopping by! now go tell my dad to take me outside. 🎾

June is a 4-year-old German Shepherd and Belgian Malinois mix rescued from Baja California. I adopted him in 2022, and he's been my little best friend ever since. He's a bit stubborn, very loyal, and sometimes reactive, but he's always trying his best. He's a good boy (most of the time), and I wouldn't trade him for anything.
